Dean of Students and Support Services

University Metropolitan Tirana has a dedicated Dean of Students page describing student orientation, engagement and support. The office's published mission emphasizes belonging, respect, personal development and a supportive learning community. Among its stated areas of attention are helping new students adapt to the university, monitoring academic progress and providing assistance when students face concerns. The page presents this work as part of the wider university experience rather than as a replacement for academic departments or administrative decisions.

The same official page describes cooperation with the Student Council, Career Counseling Office, Admissions Office and alumni network. Each has a different practical purpose. Admissions support helps people applying to UMT, career guidance is relevant to professional planning, and student representation offers channels for engagement and concerns. A student who simply asks "Who can help me settle into the university?" may need the Dean of Students rather than a programme admission page. The answer should describe the published office role without promising a particular case outcome.

UMT publishes office contact details through its official pages. For time-sensitive or personal matters, students should follow the current university contact channels instead of submitting sensitive personal records to an educational chatbot. The corpus may include public office names, but it does not contain student files, private grades or counselling conversations. This distinction makes it useful for demonstrating what a source-grounded information assistant can and cannot safely answer. Terms such as "orientation," "support," "student council" and "academic progress" provide multiple related ways to retrieve this document.
