Study Scholarships: Published Categories and Questions

Q: Does UMT describe scholarship opportunities? A: Yes. University Metropolitan Tirana's scholarship page lists categories for achievement, social circumstances, talent and other groups. Its published explanation discusses full and partial support for bachelor's studies and says scholarships apply to tuition fees rather than the administration fee. The page also describes an application process and a scholarship test. An individual award depends on the university's published criteria and decisions, not on a chatbot's recommendation.

Q: What kinds of evidence are mentioned? A: The page explains academic-performance criteria using the high-school average and State Matura results. It also mentions social scholarships supported by documentation of family or financial circumstances, talent supported by achievements or recommendations, and categories connected with teachers' children. A separate category recognizes strong academic results among current UMT bachelor's students. The details and percentages shown on a live webpage may be revised, so a 2026 snapshot must not be treated as a permanent promise.

Q: How should an applicant check eligibility? A: Find the scholarship category on the official page, read its current conditions and use the designated university process. An applicant should not send sensitive family-income records into a public educational demo. Useful search terms include "excellence scholarship," "social scholarship," "tuition discount" and "scholarship test." The chatbot should cite the source and avoid making a final eligibility decision, predicting an award or claiming a particular test date is still upcoming without a current confirmation.
