University Metropolitan Tirana: Institution Overview

University Metropolitan Tirana (UMT) is a higher-education institution in Tirana, Albania. Its official information describes an academic focus on computer science and information technology, engineering and architecture, and economics. The university presents study opportunities at several levels, from professional and bachelor's programmes to master's and doctoral study. It also highlights international cooperation, innovation, laboratories and connections with professional practice. Students seeking a general description should distinguish this university from the separate public University of Tirana; they are not the same institution.

UMT's official faculty directory identifies three faculties: Computer Science and IT, Engineering and Architecture, and Economics. The institution's English-language pages also describe programmes taught in English, alongside programmes taught in Albanian. The campus facilities page places the university in several buildings near Qyteti Studenti in Tirana. It mentions teaching rooms, administrative offices, a library and laboratories supporting different disciplines. These sources provide a useful overview, but an institution-wide description cannot replace the detailed page of a particular programme.

For a prospective student, the appropriate next step depends on the question. Programme titles and study fields belong on faculty pages; eligibility and registration belong on admissions pages; student assistance belongs on the Dean of Students page. The university's current announcements and decisions should be checked for time-sensitive information. This short overview intentionally avoids promising a particular scholarship, admission outcome, employment result or timetable. It explains what UMT says about its structure and helps users identify which official source may answer a more specific request.
