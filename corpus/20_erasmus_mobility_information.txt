Erasmus+ Mobility: Published UMT Information

UMT's Erasmus+ pages describe international mobility opportunities for students and staff. The outgoing-student page identifies International Credit Mobility under the Erasmus+ framework and explains that eligible students may study abroad for one or two semesters or pursue traineeship opportunities. It describes grant and travel support as part of the programme. These statements refer to the university's general mobility information, not to a promise that every applicant will receive funding or find an available placement in a chosen country.

For outgoing mobility, the official page lists general application materials including a passport copy, transcript of records, evidence of English-language knowledge, motivational letter, CV and Learning Agreement. The page mentions a B2 English level and explains that the Learning Agreement identifies proposed host courses and their intended recognition at UMT. It distinguishes courses to be taken abroad from those proposed for recognition after the exchange. A particular open call may add conditions, dates or partner-specific documents, so the current call remains essential.

UMT also publishes incoming-student information. Its page discusses nominations from a home university, academic documents, accommodation guidance and residence-permit preparation. It states that UMT does not provide its own dormitory service and points to other accommodation arrangements. A chatbot should not confuse incoming with outgoing procedures or claim that visa, housing and insurance questions have the same answer for every nationality. Examples for search include "Erasmus Learning Agreement," "mobility transcript," "incoming exchange housing" and "study abroad from UMT." The authoritative sources are the official UMT international-relations pages.
