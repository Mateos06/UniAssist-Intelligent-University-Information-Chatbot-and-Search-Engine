Faculty of Computer Science and IT

The Faculty of Computer Science and IT is one of the three faculties listed by University Metropolitan Tirana. Its official page presents programmes at bachelor's, master's, professional and doctoral levels. The bachelor's list includes Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Electrical Engineering with several profiles. Those electrical profiles include areas such as electronics, digital communication, control, power systems and electrical machinery. This range makes the faculty relevant to questions about software development, computing and engineering applications of technology.

At the master's level, the published faculty list includes Software Engineering, Informatics Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Renewable Energy and Management. The Artificial Intelligence master's listing identifies Data Science and Cybersecurity profiles. A two-year professional programme in Software Design and Development is also named on the faculty page. Doctoral study includes the programme in Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Systems. Applicants should consult each specific programme description to learn about learning outcomes, language, curriculum and prerequisites rather than treating a faculty list as a complete admission contract.

A student asking "Does UMT offer AI?" could refer to more than one study cycle. The assistant should therefore ask whether bachelor's, master's or doctoral study is intended, or display the relevant options without guessing. Similarly, "software" may refer to Software Engineering or the professional design-and-development programme. The official page is the grounding source for these published programme names; annual intake, admission thresholds, fees and course timetables may have separate, updated documents. The information should be checked against the current academic year's official admissions decisions before an applicant makes a final choice.
