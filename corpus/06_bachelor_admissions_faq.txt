Bachelor Admissions: Questions and Official Routes

Q: Where does an applicant start? A: University Metropolitan Tirana's English admissions page says applications to bachelor's programmes are made through the U-Albania portal. The page instructs an interested candidate to include UMT among the permitted study preferences during the application stages. It describes school completion and State Matura obligations as part of the entry framework. For an actual application, candidates must follow the current official calendar rather than assume that a portal is open every day.

Q: Are there academic or language criteria? A: The admissions page refers to the average-grade criterion set by Council of Ministers Decision No. 295 of 10 May 2019. It also mentions a language examination requirement for English-taught programmes and the integrated Architecture programme, citing Instruction No. 52 of 3 December 2015. These are references from the published page, not a new interpretation of the law. The academic senate also publishes admission decisions for particular years; candidates should compare the latest document with the general overview.

Q: Does satisfying these descriptions guarantee a place? A: No guarantee is stated here. Eligibility, quotas, rankings, registration and documentary confirmation are separate aspects of an admission process. An applicant should use the official admissions office for case-specific questions and check announcements issued for the relevant academic year. Questions such as "Where do I apply?" and "Is English required for my programme?" should receive answers grounded in these official references, with a reminder that programme-level requirements may differ.
