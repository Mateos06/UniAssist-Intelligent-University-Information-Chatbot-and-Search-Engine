Study Programmes Advertised in English

University Metropolitan Tirana has a dedicated official page for international students that lists selected English-taught programmes. It identifies bachelor's degrees in Software Engineering, Economic Informatics and Artificial Intelligence. The page describes each of these programmes as full-time and three years long. The distinction between the language of instruction and the country in which the university is located is important: students interested in studying in English should search the programme's language field, not assume that all UMT study options are in English.

The same English-language catalogue lists Master of Science programmes in Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Business Analytics and Artificial Intelligence, and Renewable Energy and Management. The page describes these master programmes as full-time and two years long. Other official faculty pages contain additional programmes, some of which may be delivered in different languages. The catalogue therefore supports a precise statement about the programmes it actually lists, not a blanket statement about the language used by every department or course.

An international applicant might ask "Which AI programme is taught in English?" or "Is Economic Informatics available in English?" A useful answer should provide the programme level and the stated format, then direct the applicant to the specific course and admissions information. Course availability, cohort arrangements and language evidence may be updated separately. This reference does not say that a student is automatically eligible or that all admission procedures can be skipped if they already speak English. Each applicant should verify current requirements and the latest official decisions before submitting documents.
