Master's Admissions: Qualifications and Supporting Papers

Q: What academic background is described for master's admission? A: University Metropolitan Tirana's admissions page says candidates for master's programmes need completed bachelor's studies. The page also refers to evidence of a foreign language through internationally recognized examinations, according to the relevant education authority's guidance. It names English, Italian, Spanish, German and French among the languages mentioned. A programme's own requirements and the latest academic-year decision should still be checked before an applicant assumes eligibility.

Q: What records appear on the official checklist? A: The published list includes a notarized bachelor's diploma, a copy of the bachelor's grade list, identification, two photographs, a CV, proof of language competence and a payment item. It also names State Matura documents and additional diploma-recognition paperwork for studies completed abroad. Because admissions instructions may change, the chatbot should report that these items appear on UMT's page rather than promise this is the final current-year checklist for every programme.

Q: Are Professional Master and Master of Science the same? A: The university describes both in its master's admission section but lists different study durations and credit totals elsewhere. A candidate should identify the exact award and programme title first. Search phrases like "master degree documents," "bachelor transcript," "CV for master" or "foreign language certificate" should lead to this entry. UniAssist must not upload application materials or decide whether a foreign diploma will be recognized; those actions require authorized institutional processes outside this educational corpus.
