Transferring Studies to UMT: Questions and Documents

Q: Does University Metropolitan Tirana publish a transfer route? A: Yes. Its English Transfer of Studies page says that it welcomes applications from students wishing to transfer, with reference to UMT regulations and the applicable legal framework. The page states that an application file can be submitted at any time, while final registration follows the official dates published by the relevant education authorities. This difference between submitting an inquiry and completing registration is important when a student asks about deadlines.

Q: Which documents are on the published checklist? A: The UMT page names a student attestation and grade list, a full curriculum outline with relevant syllabuses, notarized State Matura documents, two photographs, an identification-document copy and a registration sheet. It mentions diploma recognition for a candidate who completed high school abroad. The list can help a student prepare a question for the university, but it is not a guarantee of transfer approval or a complete legal interpretation for all cases.

Q: Will previous courses be accepted automatically? A: The public page does not give a general promise that every credit or course will be recognized. An academic review may depend on the programme and official procedures. UniAssist should therefore answer "Where can I see transfer requirements?" with this source and recommend contacting the appropriate university office about an individual file. It should never calculate a binding credit-recognition decision from the two-hundred-word corpus alone. Search terms such as "transfer," "transcript," "syllabus" and "recognition" are relevant to this document.
