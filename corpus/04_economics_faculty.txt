Faculty of Economics

The Faculty of Economics at University Metropolitan Tirana publishes programmes combining business, finance, management and technology. According to the faculty page, bachelor's options include Business Administration with Management, Entrepreneurship and Innovation, and Marketing profiles. Finance has Finance FinTech and Accounting Finance profiles. Economic Informatics is also listed, with an indication of study opportunities in English.
